# How to organize the app without overcrowding

You're right: most apps give the main screen **one clear job**. Snapchat = camera. TikTok/Instagram = video feed. Spotify is the exception (lots of cards) because *discovery* is the product. For Language Soup the product is **speaking daily with your people** — so we have to choose what the "one thing" is per screen and put the rest in clear secondary places.

Here are **three ways** to organize everything you want (daily challenges, podcast, DMs, groups, profile/stats, community/leaderboards/faces/voting).

---

## What you want to fit in

| Thing | Where it could live |
|-------|----------------------|
| Daily challenges | Your Soup and/or a dedicated entry point |
| Podcast mode | Your Soup and/or Community |
| DMs | Your Soup or a Chats tab |
| Groups | Your Soup or a Chats tab |
| Profile + stats | Profile tab |
| Community: leaderboards, faces, voting, etc. | Community tab |

---

## Option A: "Today" is the one thing (Your Soup = your day)

**Idea:** Your Soup tab has **one job**: answer "what do I do today?" So the hero is **daily challenge** (and maybe podcast as the second beat). Chats are not the main content — they're a shortcut.

**Layout:**
- **Your Soup (home):**  
  - Hero: daily challenge (big, clear).  
  - Second: podcast mode (one card).  
  - Then: one compact row — "your chats" with 2–3 group/DM previews + "see all" → full list.  
  - Stats: one line or strip ("X min this week") or "see stats" → Profile.  
  - Header: support, request, browse (unchanged).

- **Chats:** No separate tab. Tapping "see all" or a preview goes to existing **your-groups** (or a full-screen Chats list). So "conversations" are one tap away but not the main scroll.

- **Community:** One thing = **leaderboards**. Faces, voting, other features = sections below or in sub-views.

- **Profile:** One thing = **you + stats**. Groups you're in = secondary.

**Pros:** Home stays focused on habit ("today"). Chats are easy to reach but don’t compete.  
**Cons:** If someone opens the app to reply, they need one extra tap (Your Soup → see all → chat).

---

## Option B: "Conversations" are the one thing (Your Soup = chats, like WhatsApp)

**Idea:** Your Soup tab has **one job**: "who do I talk to?" So the main content is the **list of DMs + groups**. Daily challenge and podcast are the *hooks* at the top, not the scroll.

**Layout:**
- **Your Soup (home):**  
  - Top: one compact "today" strip: challenge status (one line + CTA) and podcast (one line + play). No big cards.  
  - Rest of screen: **conversation list** (DMs + groups, like the feed-style list you have now). Scroll = your people.  
  - Stats: link to Profile or one line at bottom.

- **Community:** Leaderboards = hero. Faces, voting, etc. below.

- **Profile:** You + stats = hero.

**Pros:** Opens straight into "your people." Matches the mental model "I'm here to talk." Challenge/podcast stay visible but don’t dominate.  
**Cons:** If the strip is too small, challenge/podcast might get ignored. If it’s too big, we're back to overcrowding.

---

## Option C: Four tabs — give Chats its own home

**Idea:** Split "today" and "conversations" into two tabs. So the bar is: **Community | Your Soup (today) | Chats | Profile**.

**Layout:**
- **Your Soup:**  
  - One thing = **today**: daily challenge (hero) + podcast (one card).  
  - Optional: "X groups with new messages" or 1–2 chat previews + "open chats".  
  - Stats: strip or link to Profile.

- **Chats (new tab):**  
  - One thing = **DMs + groups** (the list). No challenge, no podcast. Just conversations.  
  - Support / request / browse could live in Chats header or in Profile/settings.

- **Community:** Leaderboards = hero. Faces, voting, etc.

- **Profile:** You + stats.

**Pros:** Clearest separation: "today" vs "who to talk to." No overcrowding on one screen.  
**Cons:** One more tab; center button might need to become a normal tab (e.g. Your Soup) for balance.

---

## Recommendation

- **If** the main habit you want to reinforce is *"do the daily challenge and maybe listen"* → **Option A** keeps Your Soup as "your day" and treats chats as a clear secondary action (see all → list).
- **If** the main habit is *"open the app and talk to your groups"* → **Option B** makes conversations the one thing and keeps challenge/podcast as a thin strip so the screen isn’t crowded.
- **If** you want maximum clarity and don’t mind four tabs → **Option C** is the cleanest: Your Soup = today, Chats = people, Community = leaderboards/faces/voting, Profile = you + stats.

**Practical next step:** Pick one option and then we can adjust the current Your Soup layout (and tab bar if needed) to match — e.g. shrink or move challenge/podcast, emphasize chat list, or add a Chats tab and slim down home.
