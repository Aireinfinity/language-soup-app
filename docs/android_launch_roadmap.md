# 🚀 Road to Android Production (The "20 Tester" Rule)

You are stuck in "Internal Testing". To launch on the real Play Store, you **MUST** beat the "Boss Level" (Google's anti-spam rule for new accounts).

## The Rule: 20 Testers / 14 Days
Before you can even *apply* for Production (Open Store), you need:
1.  **20 Testers** (You have 13, need +7).
2.  Opted-in to your **Closed Testing** track (Internal doesn't count!).
3.  Active for **14 consecutive days**.

## Your Strategy: Move to "Closed Testing" NOW
"Internal" is for you and your dev team. "Closed" is for your community.

### 1. Create a Closed Testing Track
1.  Go to **Play Console** > **Testing** > **Closed testing**.
2.  Click **Create track** (Name it "Alpha").

### 2. Migrate Your 13 Testers
1.  In the new "Alpha" track, add the same **Email List** you used for Internal.
2.  **Important:** Tell your 13 testers to click the *new* link. (It looks the same, but it switches them to the Closed track).

### 3. Recruit 7 More Testers
- You need 20 total.
- Ask friends, family, or your WhatsApp group.
- **Tip:** You just need their email to opt-in. They don't need to use the app daily, they just need to satisfy the count.

## What Happens Next?
- Once you hit 20 verified testers in Closed Testing, a **14-day timer** starts in your console.
- After 14 days, a button appears: **"Apply for Production"**.
- You answer some questions, and BOOM -> You are live on the Play Store for everyone.

**Summary:**
- **Internal:** Abandon it (or keep for just you/Victor debugging).
- **Closed:** Move everyone here ASAP to start the timer.
- **Open:** Only available after you beat the Closed Testing level.
