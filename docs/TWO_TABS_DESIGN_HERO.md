# Two Tabs: Design Hero & Plan

Based on **user_interviews.md** and **LANGUAGE_SOUP_CONTEXT.md** — what users actually want, and how the two tabs should serve that.

---

## What users want (synthesis)

| Theme | What they said / need |
|-------|------------------------|
| **One clear ask** | "Likes being *asked* a question" (Ruby). Daily challenge keeps the habit (Edesanya). |
| **Bite-sized, low pressure** | "10 seconds feels doable." "It's not intimidating." "Low stakes community." (Christian, Ony) |
| **Not overwhelming** | "Too overwhelming." "Backlog anxiety." (Melissa) — Zero backlog, one hero. |
| **Community = motivation** | "I don't feel alone when learning." "Community response brings her back." "Community is the glue." (Aurelia, O'Shack) |
| **See that others are here** | "Who else is on it? Do they use it?" (Saba). Need to feel the soup is alive. |
| **Speaking, not school** | "Doesn't want it to feel like WORK." "Fun > Academic." Voice 4x more popular than text. |
| **Low-stakes, no judgment** | "A low-pressure community where I can practice speaking without feeling judged" (core value prop). |

**Activation problem:** 62.5% never message. Voice fear (Ava: "scared to send"). So: one unmissable, low-friction first step; reassurance; optional listen-before-send.

---

## Design hero (one sentence per tab)

- **Today:** *"What’s the one thing we want you to do? Speak today."* — One hero (today’s challenge), one primary CTA. Everything else supports that or is secondary.
- **Community:** *"Your people and that the soup is alive."* — Your conversations + a clear sense that others are here (pulse / say hello). No dashboard of eight blocks.

---

## Today tab — Plan

| Priority | What | Why (from users) |
|----------|------|-------------------|
| **Hero** | One big "today’s challenge" card — phrase or "1 waiting" + single CTA to open challenge queue | Ruby: being *asked*; Edesanya: daily challenge = habit; 10s doable, not overwhelming |
| **Support** | Your conversations (groups/DMs) — short list or cards so you can "respond to others" (Sydney) and get back to the challenge | Community is the glue; reply in context |
| **No** | No wall of cards. No backlog count. No "minutes spoken" as hero. | Melissa: backlog anxiety; one clear thing |
| **Optional** | Light stats or "you did X today" under the fold; support / more as small link or icon | Not competing with the one verb: speak |

**Copy vibe:** "Your turn." "One waiting." "Speak a little. Every day." — Not "Complete your daily challenge" (school).

---

## Community tab — Plan

| Priority | What | Why (from users) |
|----------|------|-------------------|
| **Hero** | Your conversations (groups, DMs) — the people you talk to | Core: "your people," not alone (O'Shack, Aurelia) |
| **Pulse** | One line that the soup is alive — e.g. "X soupers in the soup" or "say hi when you're ready" | Saba: "who else is on it?"; community on your phone |
| **Say hello** | Record + play others’ hellos — low-friction way to see/hear the community | Connection without pressure; "making new friends" (Johnny) |
| **Keep small** | Feature voting — "what we’re building" + upvote | Builds ownership; "language social media" (product learnings) |
| **Cut or move** | Big community grid, leaderboards, chat-with-native as a big block | Reduces "so many features"; can live on profile or one CTA/link |
| **No** | Eight sections fighting for attention | Nandi: worried about scale overwhelming; keep it scannable |

**Copy vibe:** "Your conversations." "Say hello." "X soupers in the soup." — Alive, human, not a settings page.

---

## Summary

- **Today:** One hero (today’s challenge), one primary action (speak). Conversations and light support in service of that.
- **Community:** Your people + pulse + say hello + feature voting. Everything else (leaderboards, native CTA, big grid) can be trimmed or moved so the tab doesn’t feel like "a lot."

Users want one clear ask, low pressure, and to feel the community is there — not a full dashboard on each tab.
