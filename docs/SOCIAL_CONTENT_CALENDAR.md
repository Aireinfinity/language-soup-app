## 🔔 How to not forget (without adding every event manually)

**Set one recurring reminder** — e.g. **Monday 9am** on your phone or in Google Calendar — titled something like **"Check social calendar"**. When it fires, open this doc and look at the next 7–14 days: any milestones, holidays, or anniversaries? Add only those to your calendar (or just post that day). You don’t have to add every single thing in advance; a **weekly check** is enough. This is also in [FOUNDER_SCHEDULE](FOUNDER_SCHEDULE.md) as a weekly anchor.

## 📱 Content Pillars (quick ref)

1. **The Hook (Daily Challenges)** — Noah + app screenshot. Builds habit/trust. (4/week)  
   - *Example:* "Everyone in the soup is failing today's challenge... can you do better?"
2. **The Vibe (Polyglot Diary)** — Noah traveling/living in multiple languages. Builds aspiration. (2/week)  
   - *Example:* "How I use 3 languages to survive a day in Budapest without a textbook."
3. **The Spark (Anti-School Rants)** — Call out traditional learning failures. Targets "Classroom Trauma." (1/week)  
   - *Example:* "Stop trying to conjugate reflexive verbs. It's killing your progress."

**Style:** Raw, authentic, walking/talking > over-edited memes.  
**Goal:** 1 video/day across TikTok/Reels/LinkedIn. 3-second hook rule; use "translanguaging" in captions when it fits.

## 📲 How to get reminders (Google Calendar)

You don't need a fancy social tool — just **Google Calendar** so you get a notification and don't forget.

1. **Open Google Calendar** (calendar.google.com or app).
2. **Create a new event** for each thing you want to be reminded of.
3. **Title:** Use something clear so you know what to post, e.g.  
   - `Post: Valentine's + languages`  
   - `Post: 1 month app anniversary`  
   - `Post: 200 users milestone`
4. **When:**  
   - For **milestones** (200 users, 1 month): set the date you expect (or the day you want to post). Add a **reminder** 1–2 days before so you can batch the content.  
   - For **holidays**: set the event on the holiday (or 1 day before) and add a reminder that morning (e.g. 9am "Post: Valentine's").
5. **Optional:** Create a calendar called **"Language Soup – Social"** and put all post reminders there so they don't clutter your main calendar. You can still get notifications.

**Example events to add right now:**

- **Feb 14 (or 13)** — `Post: Valentine's – say I love you in 5 languages` — Reminder: morning of.
- **~Feb 20–25** (adjust to your real launch date) — `Post: 1 month Language Soup anniversary` — Reminder: 2 days before.
- **When you hit 200 users** — Add an event for that week: `Post: We hit 200 users!` (or set a recurring "check user count for 200" every Monday and when you hit it, add the post event).
