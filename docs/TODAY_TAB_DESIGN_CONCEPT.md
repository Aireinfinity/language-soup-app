# Today tab — design concept

Same style as the Community tab design concept. Use this to keep the Today tab focused and to guide copy, hierarchy, and future UI changes.

**Related:** `TWO_TABS_DESIGN_HERO.md`, `APP_VISUAL_DESIGN_BRIEF.md` (daily challenge card, tactility).

---

## North star

When someone opens the tab they think: **"It's time to speak my language. The challenge is out. Let's go."** They feel **excited**, **clear**, and **not overwhelmed** — with **vocab and phrases** in view, **bite-sized** steps, and the option to **listen back like podcasts**.

---

## Today vs Community (the vibe)

| Tab | Feeling we want |
|-----|------------------|
| **Today** | **Let's talk. It's time to talk.** Get hype for the challenge. I want to do the challenge. I'm ready to speak. Exciting, happy, good vibes — scream "let's speak and get in there and learn languages." |
| **Community** | **Let's see what's going on. Learn from others.** Faces, who's active, what people said. More looking, listening, discovering. |

Today is the **speak-first** tab. Community is the **see-what's-happening, learn-from-others** space.

---

## One place only: daily challenges live on Today

**Daily challenges have one home: the Today tab.** There is no separate full-screen "challenge queue" or modal that pops up. The challenge is always there as the main action — no automatic pop-up, just open Today and the flow is right there. "Do another" and any historical picker keep you on Today and advance the inline flow (same card, next challenge). This keeps the mental model simple: *Today = where I speak and do my challenges.*

---

## Creative direction: living inside a text message / audio-first

**What we want people to feel:**
- **"What's going on with the challenge?"** → instant clarity, then **get hype** → **"I want to do the challenge."**
- **"I'm ready to speak."** — The tab should feel like it's saying: *let's talk, let's get in there, let's learn by speaking.*
- **Exciting, happy, good vibes.** Not calm or contemplative — energetic, about speaking, positive.
- **After one challenge:** *"Maybe I'll do some more."* So the flow should invite "do another" or "keep going," not "you're done, see you tomorrow."

**What we want them to see and feel (design):**
- **Living inside a text message.** The experience should feel like you're in a chat thread — recording a voice note in context, not in a separate app or studio. The challenge card and recording UI should feel like part of that conversation.
- **Super vivid, dynamic waveforms.** When you're recording (or playing back), the waveforms should feel *really* there — vivid and dynamic, so it's obvious you're recording real audio. **Audio-first design**: the UI is built around voice; waveforms and recording state are first-class, not an afterthought.
- **Recording** front and center — recording buttons, clear flows, "what's going on?" at a glance.
- **Beginner phrases and vocab** for the groups and languages you're in. Everything you need to say something is right there.
- **Good flows** — one step leads naturally to the next (see prompt → tap phrase → record → send → maybe do another).

**What we must not break:** How easy it is to record and have replies **send automatically to the right group(s)**. That ease is core.

Use this for copy, layout, and the challenge card: if something doesn't support "let's talk, get hype, I'm ready to speak," or "I'm in a message, recording audio," question it.

**Challenge card redesign (to match this vibe):** The daily challenge block should feel like the **first card on the same page** — same light/cream language as the rest of Today, not a separate full-bleed colored stage. It should feel like **part of a thread** (text-message vibe). Accent with status pill + one bold record/speak CTA. **Waveforms:** make them vivid and dynamic so recording feels real and audio-first. Recording UI (prompt, phrases, record button) lives inside that same card. No bowl decoration on the in-page card so it reads as content in the flow, not a separate widget.

## 1. One hero: "The challenge is out. Let's go."

- **The daily challenge card is the only hero** — nothing competes with it.
- Copy and layout say **"Your turn"** / **"Challenge is live"** / **"Let's go"** — not "here's a task" or "you have homework."
- **One primary action:** one obvious button (e.g. big record / speak / arrow) so the next step is unmistakable.
- **Status at a glance:** not replied / skipped / done — with color or a pill, no long sentences.

**Design intent:** The first thing they see answers: "Is there a challenge? Is it my turn? What do I do?" So it feels like "the challenge is out, let's go," not "another screen to figure out."

---

## 2. "I see it's easy to speak" — vocab and phrases up front

- **Vocab** and **phrases** are visible on or right next to the challenge card (or in the first expand/tap).
- No hunting: "I see the words, I see the sentence, I can say that."
- Optional: **hint / starter phrase** so "one short sentence" is explicit and bite-sized.

**Design intent:** Lower the bar by showing the tools (vocab + phrases) immediately. So "it's easy for me to speak the language" is supported by what's on screen, not just copy.

---

## 3. Bite-sized, not overwhelming

- **One challenge at a time** in the hero — no long list of "today's 5 challenges."
- **Groups** presented as **quick access** (e.g. chips or a short list), not a full inbox.
- **Copy:** short, punchy ("record →", "do it", "listen"), not paragraphs.
- **Progression:** "Do this one thing" then "do another" or "see your groups" — one step, then the next.

**Design intent:** One main thing per view. So it feels "bite-sized" and "awesome," not "I have so much to do."

---

## 4. "I can go through my groups" — clear but not dominant

- **Groups** are **secondary to the challenge** — reachable but not the first thing.
- **Quick overview:** e.g. "Your groups" with a few items (like Community chips) and one "see all" or "open groups."
- Tapping a group opens that chat; no need to scroll through a huge list on Today.

**Design intent:** "I can go through my groups" = I know where they are and I can open them when I want, without Today feeling like a group-management screen.

---

## 5. "I can listen back like podcasts"

- **Who responded** is visible (avatars, names, or a "replies" strip) so it feels like **people**, not just "3 replies."
- **Playback** is **one-tap** (play on a voice row/card) and **clear** (waveform, duration, maybe a "now playing" state).
- Optional: **mini player** or **expandable player** so listening feels like "I'm listening to the soup" (podcast vibe), not "I opened a message."
- **Order:** "Challenge → record → see who replied → listen" so the flow ends in listening, not in a dead end.

**Design intent:** Listening is a first-class action: easy to start, easy to see who's talking, and a bit of "podcast" feel (continuous listening, clear progress).

**Post-challenge / podcast mode:**
- After the challenge (countdown): it could be cool to surface podcast mode — e.g. "great, you did it — want to hear how other people did it today?"
- **Nothing is locked:** podcast mode is not "unlocked" only after you speak; it's always available. There should be a place to listen even if you just want to listen that day.

---

## 6. Tone: excited, ready, "let's go"

- **Buttons and CTAs:** "record →", "speak", "let's go", "do it" — action words, not "Submit" or "Complete task."
- **Empty / done states:** celebratory ("you did it", "nice one") or forward-looking ("do another", "check the group"), not "All caught up" or generic "Good job."
- **Challenge card:** bold, confident (e.g. "Challenge dropped", "Your turn"), not "Daily challenge #47."

**Design intent:** Language is "it's time to speak my language" and "let's go" — so the tone is excited and ready, not bureaucratic or school-like.

---

## 7. Hierarchy on Today (what comes first)

1. **Hero:** Daily challenge card (status + prompt + vocab/phrases + one main action).
2. **Quick access:** Your groups (or "replies" / "who spoke") so they can jump to a group or listen.
3. **Listen / podcast:** "Who responded" or "voices in the soup" for this challenge (or a clear path to Community) so "listen back like podcasts" lives here or one tap away.

**Design intent:** Challenge first, then "go to my groups" or "listen to replies," then anything else. So "I'm not overwhelmed" is built into the order of the screen.

---

## In one sentence

**Today should feel like: the one place you do daily challenges — "what's going on?" → get hype → "I want to do it" → record (vivid waveforms, audio-first, like you're in a text message) → send auto to group → "maybe I'll do some more." One clear hero, good flows, exciting and happy. So people think: "It's time to talk. I'm ready to speak."**

## Under the hood (implementation)

**Main attraction:** Record your challenge if you haven’t. When `pendingChallenges.length > 0` we show the record flow first (intro → recording → done). The two-tone hero and first section are both about “record your challenge”; tap hero or open the section to start.

**Second focus:** When the next challenge drops. When there are no pending challenges (`!hasPending`) we show “next challenge drops in” with a **live countdown**. `nextChallengeIn` (e.g. `"5h 23m"` or `"12s"`) updates every second from `nextChallengeDropAt` so the page feels dynamic.

**Data flow:**
- `checkPendingChallenges()` runs on focus and fetches today’s uncompleted challenges for the user’s groups → `setPendingChallenges`. Also calls `fetchTodayChallengeStats`, `fetchNextChallengeDropAt`, etc.
- `fetchNextChallengeDropAt()` loads the next scheduled drop from the dashboard/schedule → `setNextChallengeDropAt`. A `useEffect` turns that into human-readable `nextChallengeIn` and updates every second.
- **Order on the page:** Hero (record CTA or countdown) → pulse stats (days spoken, replies today) → “record your challenge” section (if pending) → “next challenge drops in” section (if no pending) → who replied → your groups.

**Design:** Today uses the **same design language as the Community tab**: cream background wrap, two-tone hero (turquoise left, pink right), white `redesignSection`-style cards (rounded, shadow), same section title typography. So Today feels like “Community but for daily challenges.”
